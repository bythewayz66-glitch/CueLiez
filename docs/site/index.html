CueLiez

Cue + Legal Interview Engine

Real-time signal observation and testimony cues for legal interviews. Makes inquiry more meaningful for the guilty and the innocent.

Not a lie detector. CueLiez does not detect lies. It observes behavioural and linguistic signals and inconsistencies in testimony, and cues the officer. Humans judge.

The system

  • Live and session-based observation of interview signals
  • Cues and probe suggestions — not verdicts
  • Sealed observed indicators into a trusted memory substrate
  • Consent, training, dual-control, and prohibited-use gates
