# CueLiez Demo Script / Talk Tracks

Use these as adaptable talk tracks. Keep the compliant framing every time.

## 10-second version
“CueLiez is the Legal Interview Engine. It’s not a lie detector. In real time it observes signals and testimony inconsistencies and gives the professional cues on what to clarify. Everything is sealed as observed indicators — never guilt or proof of lying — so inquiry is clearer and more auditable. Humans make the judgments.”

## 30-second version
“CueLiez helps professionals run better human-led interviews. It watches for patterns like vagueness, hedging, response shifts, and possible testimony conflicts, then surfaces cues — not verdicts — with evidence and confidence. It’s built with hard gates: consent and training in high-stakes modes, dual-control in government contexts, and prohibited-use enforcement. The record stores observed indicators with disclaimers, not findings of deception. So it supports sharper questions and fairer documentation, while keeping humans responsible for decisions.”

## 3-minute standard demo talk track

### Opening
“I’m going to show CueLiez, the Legal Interview Engine. The most important thing to know up front: **CueLiez is not a lie detector**. It doesn’t decide who’s lying or telling the truth. It gives professionals observations and cues so they can ask better questions.”

### Problem
“In interviews, people have to track details, consistency, timing, and how questions are answered — all live. Important signals can be missed, and follow-up can be inconsistent.”

### Show observations
“Here’s a synthetic interview transcript. I’ll run it through the [gov/leg/med/thrp] cue pack.”
*(Run demo; show onscreen panel.)*
“Notice the output isn’t a verdict. Each item is an observation — for example, hedging, low specificity, or a possible statement conflict — with a score, evidence, and a suggested cue. The professional reviews it and decides what to ask next.”

### Show delivery
“The same cues can be presented onscreen or as audio alerts for the professional — priority tones and scripts — so attention goes to moments worth clarifying. Again, these are prompts, not conclusions.”

### Show guardrails
“CueLiez is also built around controls. High-stakes modes require consent and training. Government mode can require dual-control before sealing high-priority cues. When we seal a package, the system forces observed-indicator status, includes disclaimers, and rejects verdict-style labels or findings.”

### Close
“So the value is clearer inquiry, consistent follow-up, and an auditable record of what was observed — not automated truth. Where would this fit best in your current interview workflow?”

## Audience-specific one-liners

- **Gov:** “CueLiez supports government interview review with consistency/timeline cues, strict gates, and dual-control — never automated security findings.”
- **Legal:** “CueLiez helps legal interviewers spot moments to clarify and document, without producing legal findings or deception verdicts.”
- **Medical:** “CueLiez can support non-diagnostic clinical communication review by cueing clarity and follow-up — never diagnosis or credibility judgments.”
- **Therapy:** “CueLiez can surface supportive clarification/pacing cues in a human-led context — never credibility, risk, or truthfulness determinations.”
