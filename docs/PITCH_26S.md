# CueLiez — 26-second description

**Timed read (~26 seconds at a steady speaking pace):**

CueLiez is the Legal Interview Engine. It is not a lie detector. In real time it observes stress-related signals, linguistic patterns, and inconsistencies in testimony, and cues the officer on what to probe next. Everything seals as observed indicators with confidence scores — never as guilt or proof of lying — so interviews get tighter for the guilty and fairer for the innocent. Hard gates enforce consent, training, and dual-control where required. We sell clearer inquiry and an auditable record, not automated truth.

---

**Even shorter (optional cut):**

CueLiez helps officers run better legal interviews. It watches for signals and testimony conflicts, suggests questions, and seals observations — not verdicts. Not a lie detector. Built for meaningful inquiry for both the guilty and the innocent.
