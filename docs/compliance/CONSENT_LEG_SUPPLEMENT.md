- **Form ID:** `cueliez-consent-leg-supp`
- **Version:** `2026.1`
- **Domain:** `leg`
- **Mode:** `interrogation`

## Purpose
Supplementary disclosure for legal/investigative interview contexts.

## Disclosures
1. **Legal/investigative context** (required, `leg_context`)
   - The operator must explain the nature of the interview and provide all notices required by applicable law, policy, and jurisdiction.
2. **Rights and required notices** (required, `leg_rights`)
   - Rights, counsel/access, warnings, voluntariness, and recording notices are governed by applicable law/policy and must be completed by the operator. This template does not provide legal advice.
3. **No legal findings** (required, `leg_no_legal_finding`)
   - CueLiez outputs are not legal findings, evidence of guilt/innocence, or determinations of deception. They may only be used as cues to guide human questioning and documentation.
4. **Documentation and review** (required, `leg_records`)
   - Observations, cues, and follow-up actions should be documented according to policy. Sealed packages preserve observed indicators and disclaimers, not verdicts.
