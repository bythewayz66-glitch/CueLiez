## Purpose
Supplementary disclosure for clinical interview support (non-diagnostic).

## Disclosures
1. **Not a medical device / not diagnostic** (required, `med_not_device`)
   - CueLiez is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment recommendations, or clinical determinations. It does not assess symptom validity or patient credibility.
2. **Communication support only** (required, `med_support`)
   - Any cues are intended to support clear communication and careful human-led history-taking. Clinical concerns must be addressed through qualified clinical judgment and applicable pathways.
3. **Privacy and health information** (required, `med_privacy`)
   - Health/session information must be handled according to applicable privacy laws, policies, and professional obligations (for example, consent, confidentiality, and record rules as applicable).
4. **Optional feature modalities** (required, `med_modalities`)
   - If audio/prosody, visual, or physiological feature summaries are used, they must be lawfully obtained, consent-governed, and interpreted as non-diagnostic observations only.
