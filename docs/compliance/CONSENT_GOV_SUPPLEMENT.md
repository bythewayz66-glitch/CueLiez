# CueLiez Supplementary Consent/Disclosure — Government / High-Security

- **Form ID:** `cueliez-consent-gov-supp`
- **Version:** `2026.1`
- **Domain:** `gov`
- **Mode:** `government`

## Purpose
Supplementary disclosure for government/high-security interview contexts.

## Disclosures
1. **Authority, jurisdiction, and scope** (required, `gov_authority`)
   - The operator must identify the lawful authority, jurisdiction, scope, and applicable policy for the session. CueLiez does not determine authority or legality.
2. **Dual-control and oversight** (required, `gov_dual_control`)
   - Government-mode sessions may require dual-control acknowledgement for high-priority cues before sealing records. Oversight and review follow applicable policy.
3. **Records handling** (required, `gov_records`)
   - Records are handled according to applicable government policy, classification, retention, and disclosure rules. This form does not override those rules.
4. **No automated security findings** (required, `gov_no_clearance`)
   - CueLiez outputs must not be used for automated clearance, access, detention, or security findings.
