## Purpose
General informed disclosure for CueLiez-assisted interview support.

## Disclosures
1. **Purpose** (required, `g_purpose`)
   - CueLiez is used to support a human-led interview by observing behavioural/linguistic signals and testimony consistency patterns and presenting them as cues for professional review.
2. **Not a lie detector / no verdicts** (required, `g_not_detector`)
   - CueLiez is not a lie detector, not a polygraph, and not a truth machine. It does not determine whether anyone is lying, truthful, guilty, or innocent. Outputs are probabilistic observations/cues for human judgment, not findings.
3. **No automated decisions** (required, `g_no_automated_decisions`)
   - CueLiez outputs must not be used as the sole or automated basis for detention, charging, clearance, diagnosis, treatment decisions, employment/immigration/custody determinations, or other legal/clinical decisions.
4. **Session data and records** (required, `g_data_recording`)
   - Session information, observations, and operator notes may be recorded, stored, and reviewed as permitted by applicable law, policy, and the purpose of the session. Sealed records preserve observed indicators with context and disclaimers, not verdicts.
5. **Human review and questions** (required, `g_human_review`)
   - A qualified human professional is responsible for interpreting cues, asking questions, and making decisions. You may ask questions about the process as permitted by applicable law/policy.
6. **Rights and voluntariness (jurisdiction/policy dependent)** (required, `g_rights_voluntariness`)
   - Any rights, voluntariness, refusal, withdrawal, counsel/access, and consequence information depends on the session type, jurisdiction, and applicable law/policy. The operator must provide required notices and follow applicable procedures before relying on this consent.
