# CueLiez Mission

**CueLiez** = **Cue** + **LIE** (**L**egal **I**nterview **E**ngine)

## Mission

Make legal interviews more meaningful for the guilty and the innocent alike by giving officers real-time **observations** of behavioural and linguistic signals and testimony inconsistency — never automated findings of truthfulness.

CueLiez exists so that high-stakes interviews are driven by clearer questions, faster notice of inconsistency, and a sealed record of *what was observed*, not by gut feel alone and not by a machine that claims to know who is lying.

## What we build

1. **Observation, not verdict** — The system notices patterns and conflicts; humans judge.
2. **Cues, not convictions** — Output is prompts and flags for the officer to use or discard.
3. **Sealed indicators** — Session artifacts enter trusted memory as observed indicators with confidence, model identity, and disclaimers — never as guilt, diagnosis, or clearance decisions.
4. **Hard gates** — Consent, training, dual-control (where required), and prohibited-use enforcement are part of the product, not optional policy text.

## What we refuse

- To be described or sold as a lie detector, truth machine, or polygraph substitute
- Automated detention, charging, clearance, or clinical diagnosis from our signals
- Definitive claims that a person "is lying"

## Success

CueLiez succeeds when an interview produces a fairer, tighter record of inquiry — and when both the process and the sealed package can stand scrutiny because they never pretended to detect lies.
